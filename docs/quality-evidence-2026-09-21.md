# September 21: evidence and publication focus

This release addresses three specific weaknesses: examples without executable
records, claims of review that exceed visible accountability, and a publication
structure broader than its collection. It does not establish AdSense eligibility.

## What changed

- Two existing guides now publish original synthetic fixtures, actual executed
  program outputs, source code, reproduction instructions and limits. Sixteen
  cases across invoice validation and document selection. Node v24.19.0 executed
  the dependency-free runner. No commercial model, Excel application, customer
  data, interview or real authorization service was tested.
- Each output includes SHA-256 hashes of inputs and runner. Expected fixture
  outcomes are visible. The rules were designed alongside the cases; passing
  them is not held-out or independent validation.
- Homepage: one lead, the preserved static three-story Canadian section, three
  experiments/worksheets and three beginner guides. No repeated featured story
  across these groups. Structured-data list matches the displayed collection.
- Navigation emphasizes Canadian policy, practical use, evaluation and the full
  archive. Existing category URLs remain reachable through article metadata.
- About identifies the organization byline's limits and points to concrete
  evidence. It explicitly says individual editor identities/credentials are
  not published. No identity or experience was invented.
- Article badges now link to sources, editorial process and (where available)
  data/code/results, rather than displaying a self-awarded review checkmark.
- Fixed route-change scrolling that discarded explicit section fragments.

## Guidance used

Google AdSense content and user experience:
https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/10015918?hl=en

The relevant recommendations are original reader value, avoiding repetitive
content, accurate navigation and a useful experience. They are not a numeric
article-count or word-count approval formula.

Microsoft SUMIFS reference, checked for the existing spreadsheet exercise:
https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/functions/sumifs-function

Our JavaScript sums are not execution of that Excel function. The experiment
isolates input validation; its results cannot verify Excel behavior.

## Verification and limits

- Public-source audit: 31 distinct external citations, no URL problems reported
  on September 21. URL validity alone does not prove every article claim.
- Preview checked at desktop width and in real 390-pixel iframe viewports for
  the homepage and invoice guide. Menu opening and section-fragment landing
  checked; experiment table fits its 351-pixel content area without overflow.
  This is responsive-width QA, not physical-phone or Safari testing.
- Both publication targets retain the 16-article eligibility gate and disabled
  ads. Drafts were not promoted to enlarge the collection.
- Named human accountability, real audience demand, expert review and independent
  field research remain unestablished by these changes. No AdSense approval or
  complete claim-by-claim audit of all 16 articles is asserted by this release.
